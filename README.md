# Samsung Innovation Campus — GenAI Course Tasks

This repository collects projects completed as tasks during the Generative AI (GenAI) course by **Samsung Innovation Campus (SIC) at my college**.

## Course work

### Day 1 — NEXUS HR Knowledge Assistant
A Streamlit retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) learning demo for searching HR policy documents with semantic retrieval.

[Open the Day 1 project](Day%201%20-%20RAG%20Knowledge%20Assistant/)

### Day 2 — Storybound Library
A book discovery demo where readers describe a story and receive matching suggestions from a sample catalog.

[Open the Day 2 notebook](Day%202%20-%20Storybound%20Library/Storybound_Library.ipynb)

### Day 3 — Prompt Evaluator
An LLM-as-a-judge tool that scores and improves prompts against six prompt-writing criteria.

[Open the Day 4 notebook](Day%203%20-%20Prompt%20Evaluator/Prompt_Evaluator_ngrok.ipynb)

### Day 4 — Student Portfolio
An AI-assisted portfolio builder personalized with my education, skills, projects, and contact links.

[Open the Day 5 notebook](Day%204%20-%20Student%20Portfolio/Yashwanth_Student_Portfolio_ngrok.ipynb)

> Storybound's catalog and request flow are sample data. The ngrok links generated by the Day 4 and Day 5 notebooks are temporary demo URLs, not permanent deployments.
